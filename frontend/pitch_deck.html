OPERATIONAL BOTTLENECK

Disaster information is fragmented.

Relevant information exists across different feeds, weather forecasts, and orbital catalogs. Operators must manually cross-reference data during time-critical events.

Disconnected Data Streams
Seismic telemetry, thermal fire points, meteorological forecasts, and satellite orbital elements reside in disparate formats without unified geospatial correlation.
Uncoordinated Tasking Requests
Optical satellites risk being tasked over heavy cloud-shrouded monsoon zones, resulting in unusable obscured imagery and depleted mission duty cycles.
THE OPERATOR'S CHALLENGE
"When an emergency unfolds, the decision is not merely knowing a disaster occurred—it is knowing which satellite can see it next and which sensor modality can penetrate prevailing weather."

SENSOR MODALITY MATCHING

Different disasters require different observations.

SkyWindow aligns the physical characteristics of each hazard with the optimal Earth observation sensor payload.

HYDROLOGICAL HAZARD
Floods & Inundation
SYNTHETIC APERTURE RADAR (SAR)
Active microwave pulses penetrate cloud cover and rain to detect surface water specular reflection and delineate flood perimeters.
ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARD
Wildfires & Heatwaves
THERMAL INFRARED (TIR) & SWIR
Shortwave and Thermal Infrared bands detect active combustion fronts through smoke and measure fire radiative power.
GEOLOGICAL HAZARD
Earthquakes & Landslides
HIGH-RES OPTICAL & InSAR
Interferometric SAR detects sub-centimeter surface displacement; high-resolution optical assesses structural damage.
