THE MISSION DILEMMA

When disaster strikes, information arrives fragmented.

Emergency response teams and satellite mission directors operate in disconnected data silos with no unified intelligence layer connecting incident detection to orbital tasking.

1. Data Silo Overload
Seismic feeds (USGS), thermal fire hotspots (FIRMS), meteorological forecasts (ECMWF), and orbital catalogs (CelesTrak) exist in disparate formats without mutual geospatial correlation.
2. Blind Optical Tasking & Wasted Passes
Optical satellites are routinely tasked over flooded or cyclone-ravaged zones only to capture 100% cloud obscuration—wasting critical satellite power (Wh), onboard storage (GB), and precious imaging windows.
3. Missing Decision Support Layer
Current platforms stop at showing a static marker on a map. They fail to calculate: Which sensor modality is required? When is the next pass? What critical infrastructure is compromised?
